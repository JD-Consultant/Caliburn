## 第 1 題:任務句(task statement)撰寫規範

### 1a. O\*NET —— 最完整、最可直接當「句式規則」教材

**權威文件:** National Center for O\*NET Development, *Appendix B: Task Writing Guidelines*
URL: https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/GreenTask_AppB.pdf(引 Cunningham 2000;O\*NET 綠色職業任務更新專案附錄)

**官方定義(原文):**
> "Task statements are exemplars of the types of activities workers in an occupation may perform. **Tasks are typically conceptualized as the smallest unit of activity with a meaningful outcome.**"

→ 這是「一條 task 的粒度」的官方判準:**有意義產出的最小活動單位**。

**任務句結構(官方三段式,原文):**
> Each task statement should follow the following basic format:
> **Action (Behavior) > Object of the Action > Purpose/Result, Enabler, and/or Context**

| 成分 | 官方問句 | 官方描述(原文重點) |
|---|---|---|
| **Action(行為)** | What action does the worker perform on, to or with the object? | "indicated by a verb, which introduces the task statement. The verb should be **as specific and concrete as possible**. The verb tense should assume a **plural subject in the present tense**." |
| **Object(受詞)** | What object is affected? | "the noun of the task statement… will usually include **Things, Data or People**." |
| **Purpose/Result** | What is the intended outcome? | "may be implicit or explicit. When explicit, usually introduced by a **'to' statement**." |
| **Enabler** | 如何支援達成 | 工具/方法/資訊來源;"introduced with words such as **using, referring to, relying on, following, based on**." |
| **Context** | 執行條件/場景 | "generally **rare** in task statements." |

**單一 vs 複合(官方原則,原文):**
> "Generally, it is best **not to write complex, compound task statements** involving multiple action and purpose components. When task statements are too complex, consider writing multiple task statements, rather than a single comprehensive statement."

**Task Writing Style —— 逐條句式禁令(官方原文,可直接當 checklist):**
- ✅ **Assume a plural subject**:"Complete assignments" 對,"Completes assignments" 錯。
- 縮寫首次出現要拼全+括號標縮寫。
- ❌ **不用分號**:"A semicolon implies that there are two distinct tasks in one statement." → 拆兩句,或用 and/or 連。
- ❌ 不用括號(縮寫例外)。
- ❌ **不用 "e.g." 或 "i.e."** → 改用 "such as"。
- ❌ **不用 "and/or"** → 用 "or"。
- ⚠️ **"or" 應比 "and" 更常用**:"When 'and' is used, job incumbents may believe they need to do every aspect of the task to endorse it. 'Or' is more inclusive."
- "such as" 清單用 **and** 收尾;"using" 清單用 **or** 收尾。
- ❌ 不用斜線 "/" → 改 and/or。
- ❌ **不用 "in order to"** → 用 "to"(更簡潔)。
- ❌ **不寫時間相依**("do this, then do that")→ 時間相依=兩條任務。
- ❌ 不用 "etc."。
- ❌ **用 "use" 不用 "utilize"**("utilize… may strike readers as pretentious jargon")。

**好壞範例(官方原句):**
- 好:`Order supplies such as paper, pencils and erasers, using telephone or computer.`(such as 用 and,using 用 or,動詞開頭複數現在式)
- 壞型:含分號、含 e.g./i.e.、含 "in order to"、含時間相依("...then...")。

### 1b. UK NOS —— performance criteria 寫法(不同哲學:第二人稱、標準導向)

**權威文件:** UK Standards & Frameworks Panel, *Quality Criteria for development of National Occupational Standards*(Revised August 2023, effective 1 April 2023)
URL: https://ukstandards.org.uk/media/nfmcz5ir/nos-quality-criteria-11-09-23.pdf

**Performance criteria(§5.7,官方原文):**
> "Performance criteria answer the question: **'what does an individual need to do or ensure happens in order to carry out the function to a satisfactory standard?'**"
> "Taken together, the performance criteria should be capable of **distinguishing between satisfactory and unsatisfactory performance**…"
> "Each performance criterion should **start with an active verb in the second person singular** and be able to follow the introductory phrase: **'You must be able to...'**."
> "Each performance criteria should clearly and concisely specify what the individual… needs to do or ensure happens **and the standard of performance that is satisfactory**."

**契合度差異(重要對照):**
- O\*NET task:**第三人稱複數、現在式、描述性**(worker 做什麼),`Complete assignments`。
- NOS performance criterion:**第二人稱單數、能力聲明式、內含品質門檻**,`You must be able to... refer it promptly to the relevant department`。

**Contingency criteria(NOS 特有,官方原文):**
> "Performance criteria also need to cover likely contingencies… For example, 'when the query is outside your area of responsibility, refer it promptly to the relevant department'." —— 條件子句(when...)+主動詞。

**NOS title 也有句式規則(§5.5,官方原文):**
> "A NOS title should start with an **active verb (e.g., 'Encourage' not 'Encouraging')** which accurately describes the nature of the function, followed by the **object of the verb** (e.g., 'innovation')."

## 第 2 題:產出(output / deliverable)

**關鍵發現:主流職能體系「沒有」把「工作產出/deliverable」設為獨立正式欄位。** 產出是**內含**在任務句或功能敘述裡:

- **O\*NET**:產出藏在 task 結構的 "Purpose/Result"(意圖產出,以 "to..." 引導),不是獨立欄位。且 task 本身定義=**"smallest unit of activity with a meaningful outcome"**——outcome/產出是任務成立的必要條件,但不獨立列。
- **UK NOS**:以 **function / key purpose** 表達「要達成什麼」。§3.2 官方原文:"Functional analysis starts with a **key purpose – a concise, yet comprehensive statement of what is involved in the area of work – the common goal to be achieved.**" 產出=功能的達成,不獨立成欄。
- **ESCO / SFIA / EQF**:無 deliverable 欄位;SFIA level 5 essence 提及 "delivering significant work outcomes, from analysis through execution to evaluation",但仍是責任層級描述,非產出清單。

**對教材的判準含意:** 若 Caliburn 要獨立「產出欄」,國際上**無現成官方欄位可對映**,屬於超出 O\*NET/NOS 慣例的設計。最接近的權威做法是 **O\*NET 的 "to + 意圖結果"** 句式:把產出寫成任務的可驗證結果("...to produce X","...resulting in X")。可據此定判準:**產出必須是名詞化、可交付、可查核的物件(Things/Data),不是活動本身**。

## 第 3 題:行為指標(observable behavior)

### 3a. 官方「可觀察/可量測」的核心規則來自 Bloom 修訂版

**權威原著:** David R. Krathwohl (2002), *A Revision of Bloom's Taxonomy: An Overview*, **Theory Into Practice, 41(4), 212–218**, College of Education, Ohio State University(Anderson & Krathwohl 2001 修訂版之官方 overview)。
下載頁: https://cmapspublic2.ihmc.us/rid=1Q2PTM7HL-26LTFBX-9YN8/Krathwohl%202002.pdf

**最關鍵的「壞指標長什麼樣」——官方原文(Airasian & Miranda 同期,pp. 249–250):**
> "To avoid this confusion, we have suggested that the 19 cognitive processes… (or… the six process categories) should be used as the **verbs** when stating objectives. **Ambiguous verbs such as 'state,' 'list,' 'demonstrate,' and so on, should be used with great care because many of these terms are more applicable to assessment than to learning.**"

Krathwohl 對 "understand" 的官方診斷(p. 214,原文):
> "…when teachers say they want the student to '**really' understand, they mean anything from Comprehension to Synthesis.**"

→ **不可觀察的壞指標**:動詞是 `understand / know / appreciate / be aware of / really understand`——因為它可涵蓋從記憶到創造的任何層次,**觀察者無法判定達成**。這是 Bloom 團隊當年連 "understand" 都一度不敢當類別名的原因。

**好指標的官方做法:用可觀察的認知歷程動詞。** 六大歷程 + 19 個次歷程(Krathwohl 2002, Table 3,原文定義):

| 類別 | 官方定義(原文) | 可觀察動詞(次歷程) |
|---|---|---|
| **Remember** | "Retrieving relevant knowledge from long-term memory." | Recognizing, Recalling |
| **Understand** | "Determining the meaning of instructional messages…" | Interpreting, Exemplifying, Classifying, Summarizing, Inferring, Comparing, Explaining |
| **Apply** | "Carrying out or using a procedure in a given situation." | Executing, Implementing |
| **Analyze** | "Breaking material into its constituent parts and detecting how the parts relate…" | Differentiating, Organizing, Attributing |
| **Evaluate** | "Making judgments based on criteria and standards." | Checking, Critiquing |
| **Create** | "Putting elements together to form a novel, coherent whole or make an original product." | Generating, Planning, Producing |

**「行為 vs 意圖」的官方警訊(可直接當判準):**
Raths(同期 p. 236)引 Merrill 1971 的 **push-down principle**:"Learners have an innate tendency to reduce the cognitive load… a learner will attempt to perform a given response at the lowest possible level." → 觀察者看到「看似高難度任務」時,學習者可能實際在低層次操作。故 Bloom 團隊**只分類「意圖」(objectives)而非「實際行為」**。對指標教材的含意:行為指標要寫**可外部觀察的動作**,別假設內在歷程。

### 3b. UK NOS 的「行為(Behaviours)」欄——官方定義與範例

NOS Quality Criteria §5.12(選填元件,官方原文):
> "Behaviours… define **how an individual undertakes activities and the way in which employees respond to specific circumstances or situations in the workplace.**"
> 官方範例:"'make time available to support others'" / "'clearly agree what is expected of others and hold them to account'" / "'take timely decisions that are realistic for the situation'".

→ NOS 的行為句式:**可觀察的、對情境的回應方式**,通常動詞開頭祈使句。

## 第 4 題:K / S / A(/ O)三分——各體系官方定義原文

### 4a. O\*NET Content Model —— K/S/A 官方區分

**權威頁:** https://www.onetcenter.org/content.html(The O\*NET Content Model)/ 描述子瀏覽 https://www.onetonline.org/find/descriptor/browse/
(以下為官方 Content Model 定義,建議二次核對原頁逐字)

- **Knowledge**:"sets of **principles and facts** applying in general domains"(組織化的事實與原理原則;透過教育與經驗習得)。
- **Skills**:"**developed capacities** that facilitate learning or the more rapid acquisition of knowledge"(透過訓練/經驗發展的能力;35 項,分 content/process/social/complex problem solving/technical/systems/resource management 七類)。
- **Abilities**:"**enduring attributes** of the individual that influence performance"(較穩定的個人稟賦;分 Cognitive 21 / Physical 9 / Psychomotor 10 / Sensory 12 四大類)。

**K/S/A 三分的官方判準(教材可用):**
- **Ability = 較穩定、天賦性的稟賦**(如反應時間、動作協調),影響學得多快多好。
- **Skill = 可透過訓練發展的能力**,建立在 ability 之上。
- **Knowledge = 事實與原理原則的體系**。
- O\*NET 沒有把 "Attitude" 放進 K/S/A;態度性內容在 **Work Styles**(見第 8 題)。所以嚴格說 O\*NET 是 KSA + Work Styles,不是 KSAO 單一欄。

### 4b. 學術原點:KSAO 框架

「KSAO」(Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, Other characteristics)是工業組織心理學/工作分析(job analysis)的標準術語,**"O"(Other)** 涵蓋態度、人格、興趣、價值等非 K/S/A 特質。O\*NET 的 Content Model 即此傳統的政府級實作(K/S/A 三欄 + Work Styles/Work Values 承接 "O")。

### 4c. ESCO —— knowledge vs skill/competence 二分(官方原文)

**權威頁(escopedia):**
- Knowledge: https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/escopedia/escopedia/knowledge
- Skill: https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/escopedia/escopedia/skill
- Competence: https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/escopedia/escopedia/competence
(定義直接對齊 **EQF 建議書 2008/C 111/01** 的官方定義)

**Knowledge(官方原文):**
> "Knowledge means the **outcome of the assimilation of information through learning**. Knowledge is the **body of facts, principles, theories and practices** that is related to a field of work or study."

**Skill(官方原文):**
> "Skill means the **ability to apply knowledge and use know-how to complete tasks and solve problems**."
> 分 **cognitive**(logical, intuitive and creative thinking)與 **practical**(manual dexterity and the use of methods, materials, tools and instruments)。

**Competence(官方原文):**
> "Competence means the **proven ability to use knowledge, skills and personal, social and/or methodological abilities**, in work or study situations and in professional and personal development."

**skill vs competence 的官方區分(escopedia 原文):**
> "The term **skill** refers typically to the **use of methods or instruments in a particular setting and in relation to defined tasks**. The term **competence** is broader and refers typically to the ability of a person — **facing new situations and unforeseen challenges** — to use and apply knowledge and skills in an **independent and self-directed** way."

**ESCO 撰寫慣例(超重要句式規則,官方原文):**
> 「In ESCO v1, the terms used to describe **knowledge** concepts **do not make use of action verbs** and do not include the verb *know* or the noun *knowledge* — for example, ESCO uses '**fermentation process**' instead of 'Know fermentation process' or 'Knowledge of fermentation process'.」

→ **判準:knowledge 寫成名詞化的領域概念(不加動詞、不加「知道/知識」);skill 才用動詞/動作短語表達。** 這正好對映 Bloom 的「名詞=知識維度、動詞=認知歷程維度」二分。

### 4d. Bloom 修訂版的「知識維度」四型(Krathwohl 2002, Table 2,原文)——可細化「知識欄」判準

- **A. Factual Knowledge** — "The basic elements that students must know…"(術語、具體細節)。
- **B. Conceptual Knowledge** — "The interrelationships among the basic elements within a larger structure…"(分類、原理原則、理論/模型/結構)。
- **C. Procedural Knowledge** — "How to do something; methods of inquiry, and criteria for using skills, algorithms, techniques, and methods."(含 Cc:**知道何時該用哪個程序**)。
- **D. Metacognitive Knowledge** — "Knowledge of cognition in general as well as awareness and knowledge of one's own cognition."(策略知識、任務知識、自我知識)。

Krathwohl 的核心洞見(p. 212–213,原文):目標句 = **名詞(知識)+ 動詞(認知歷程)** 兩維;原版把兩者混在 Knowledge 一類是「anomaly」,修訂版拆成兩軸的 **Taxonomy Table**(4 知識型 × 6 歷程 = 24 格)。

## 第 5 題:級別體系——「怎麼判第幾級」的官方判準語言

### 5a. SFIA —— 7 級 responsibility(最操作化的層級判準)

**權威頁:** https://sfia-online.org/en/about-sfia/how-sfia-works ;各級 https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9/responsibilities/level-N

**四個 generic attributes(官方定義,原文):**
- **Autonomy**: "The level of **independence, discretion and accountability for results** in your role."
- **Influence**: "The **reach and impact** of your decisions and actions, both within and outside the organisation."
- **Complexity**: "The **range and intricacy** of tasks and responsibilities…"
- **Knowledge**: "The **depth and breadth of understanding** required to perform and influence work effectively."
- 另有 **Business skills / behavioural factors**(12 維:collaboration, communication, creativity, decision-making, digital mindset, leadership, learning & development, planning, problem-solving, adaptability, improvement mindset, security/privacy/ethics)。

**七級一詞概括(官方):**
1 Follow · 2 Assist · 3 Apply · 4 Enable · 5 Ensure/advise · 6 Initiate/influence · 7 Set strategy, inspire, mobilise
官方原則:"Each level represents increasing responsibility, accountability and impact in the workplace."

**逐級判準語言範例(直接可當「怎麼判級」教材):**

**Level 3 – Apply**(官方原文):
- Essence: "Performs varied tasks, sometimes complex and non-routine, using standard methods and procedures. Works under general direction, exercises discretion, and manages own work within deadlines."
- Autonomy: "Works under **general direction**… has work reviewed at agreed milestones."
- Influence: "Works with and influences team decisions…"
- Complexity: "Performs a range of work, sometimes complex and non-routine, in varied environments."
- Knowledge: "Applies knowledge of a range of role-specific practices… within defined boundaries."

**Level 5 – Ensure, advise**(官方原文):
- Essence: "Provides **authoritative guidance** in their field and works under **broad direction**. Accountable for delivering significant work outcomes, from analysis through execution to evaluation."
- Autonomy: "Works under **broad direction**. Work is **self-initiated**…"
- Influence: "**Influences critical decisions** in their domain…"
- Complexity: "…**fundamental principles in a range of unpredictable contexts**."
- Knowledge: "…interpret complex situations and offer authoritative advice… **in-depth expertise** in specific fields."

→ **判級判準 = 沿四軸看措辭升階**:autonomy(closely supervised→general direction→broad direction→sets strategy)、influence(team→domain→org→industry)、complexity(routine→non-routine→unpredictable→abstract)、knowledge(role-specific→in-depth→strategic)。

### 5b. EQF —— 8 級,三維描述子

**權威頁:** Europass, *Description of the eight EQF levels* — https://europass.europa.eu/en/description-eight-eqf-levels(依 EQF 建議書 2008/2017)

**每級三維(官方原文用語):**
- **Knowledge** — "theoretical and/or factual."
- **Skills** — "cognitive (…logical, intuitive and creative thinking) and practical (…manual dexterity and the use of methods, materials, tools and instruments)."
- **Responsibility and autonomy** — "the ability of the learner to **apply knowledge and skills autonomously and with responsibility**."

8 級由 Level 1(基礎/受監督)升到 Level 8(最前沿/權威自主/創新)。EQF 是**學習成果導向**的「翻譯工具」,三維隨級遞增。

### 5c. 與「6 級制」對映的概念

- EQF/SFIA 皆非 6 級(EQF 8、SFIA 7)。台灣 iCAP 職能級別為 **6 級**。
- **官方對映語言**在三者共通的**軸**上:autonomy(自主程度)、complexity/context(任務複雜與情境可預測性)、influence/scope(影響範圍)、knowledge depth(知識深廣)。教「怎麼判 N 級」時,用這四軸的**升階措辭**當跨體系共同尺,比硬套級數對照更穩。
- 註:EQF↔iCAP 官方無正式對映表;跨體系級數換算須謹慎,建議以「軸的描述語」而非「級號」對齊。

## 第 6 題:粒度與數量慣例(一職業幾條 duty、一 duty 幾條 task)

**誠實結論:主流體系「沒有」官方規定的數量區間。** 只有**粒度定義**,無「X 條」建議:

- **O\*NET**:task = **"the smallest unit of activity with a meaningful outcome"**(GreenTask_AppB,原文)。給的是「最小有意義產出單位」的**質性**門檻,不是數量。O\*NET 各職業的 task 數量是實證產出的結果,非規範。
- **UK NOS**(§5.1,官方原文):"**Each NOS should cover a single function** which can be performed by an individual." → 一份 NOS = **一個功能**(單一函數)。功能來自 functional analysis 三階(§3.2):key purpose → main functions → discrete functions。**粒度靠「功能可否由一人執行」界定,不設條數。**
- **NOS 的 sub-section/element**(§5.7 原文):一個功能(如 "Plan, organise, monitor, and evaluate…")可拆成數個 element(Plan/Organise/Monitor/Evaluate),但無官方上限。
- **ESCO / SFIA / EQF**:無「每職業幾條」的官方數量建議。

**對教材含意:** 「一 duty 幾條 task」屬**體系自定**。可給的權威判準是**質性**的:(a) task 到「最小有意義產出」為止;(b) 一 duty/function 應「可由一人執行、對映一個 key purpose 下的 main function」;(c) O\*NET 反覆強調**寧可拆多條、不要寫複合句**。數量區間若要訂,是 Caliburn 的設計選擇,國際上無現成權威數字可引。

## 第 7 題:品質檢核(怎麼審一條寫好的內容)

### 7a. UK NOS —— 最完整的官方品質判準(Quality Criteria 全 25 頁)

**強制元件(§5.2,原文):** URN;NOS title;NOS overview;**performance criteria**;**specification of knowledge and understanding**;technical data。
**選填元件(§5.3):** scope/range;values;behaviours;skills;glossary;links;external links。

**逐條品質判準(官方原文,可直接當審核清單):**
- **Performance criteria(§5.7)**:合起來要能「distinguish between satisfactory and unsatisfactory performance」、「cover all the aspects which are critical」、每條「achievable by a competent individual」、第二人稱主動詞、內含 satisfactory standard、涵蓋 contingencies。
- **Knowledge & Understanding(§5.8)**:回答 "what does an individual need to know and understand… to perform this function consistently to the required standard?";**只納入對有效表現「essential」者**(不放無關知識)。
- **Scope/Range(§5.10)**:"should **only be used where different circumstances… have a critical impact** on performance. Scope or range should **not** be used simply to provide illustrative examples"(舉例澄清用 glossary,不用 range)。且區分:**Range = 所有必須符合的條件;Scope = 個人可能遇到的不同變項**(官方 §Glossary)。
- **Values(§5.11)** / **Behaviours(§5.12)** / **Skills(§5.13)**:各有官方定義與範例(見前)。
- **無歧視(§5.19)**:內容與用字須 free from direct or indirect discrimination。
- **文字品質(§5.20)**:"concise, clear and appropriate for the individuals carrying out the function"(可能需 professional language review)。
- **證據本位(§2.1)**:每份 NOS 須反映 current effective practice,經代表性雇主與 SME 共識。

**流程級品質保證:** 四國諮詢 → 功能圖 → Draft NOS(SQA Accreditation 品保)→ Part A/B 證據 → Final NOS 簽核。另有專門文件 *Setting the Standard – Principles for Writing NOS*(§附錄提及,為 NOS 撰寫原則專書)。

### 7b. O\*NET —— 一致性導向的品質判準

O\*NET 品質核心 = **結構+風格一致性**(見第 1 題整份 Task Writing Guidelines)。引 Cunningham 2000(原文):"Task writing… is an art, and no two writers will produce exactly the same results. It is essential, however, that writers **follow common guidelines** in order to maintain some uniformity."(資料更新程序見 Dierdorff & Norton 2011, *Summary of Procedures for O\*NET Task Updating*, https://www.onetcenter.org/reports/TaskUpdating.html)

### 7c. Bloom Taxonomy Table —— 對齊性(alignment)品質判準

Krathwohl 2002 / Anderson & Miranda(原文):把 **objectives、instructional activities、assessments** 各自放進 Taxonomy Table 24 格,**三者落在同一格 = 完全對齊(good quality)**;錯格 = 診斷出品質問題(如「有活動無目標」「有目標無評量」)。教材可借此教:**任務—指標—級別三者要落在同一認知層/知識型,才算內部一致。**

## 第 8 題:通用態度 / 軟技能官方清單

### 8a. O\*NET Work Styles(態度/人格性特質的官方清單)

**權威頁:** https://www.onetonline.org/find/descriptor/browse/1.C (Work Styles);最新修訂 *2024 No. 090 Revisiting the Work Styles Domain*, https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/Work_Styles_New.pdf
定義=「personal characteristics that can affect how well someone performs a job」。官方清單(含定義,建議二次核對逐字):
- **Achievement/Effort** — 設定並維持有挑戰性的成就目標、努力精熟任務。
- **Persistence** — "persistence in the face of obstacles."
- **Initiative** — "a willingness to take on responsibilities and challenges." / 主動承擔額外職責。
- **Leadership** — "a willingness to lead, take charge, and offer opinions and direction."
- **Cooperation** — "being pleasant with others… and displaying a good-natured, cooperative attitude."
- **Concern for Others / Empathy** — 對他人需求與感受敏感、樂於協助。
- **Social Orientation** — 偏好與人共事而非獨自。
- **Self-Control** — 保持冷靜、控制情緒、避免攻擊行為。
- **Stress Tolerance** — 接受批評、冷靜有效地處理高壓。
- **Adaptability/Flexibility** — 對變化(正/負向)開放、能應變。
- **Dependability** — "being reliable, responsible, and dependable, and fulfilling obligations."
- **Attention to Detail** — 對細節仔細、徹底完成工作。
- **Integrity** — "being honest and ethical."
- **Independence** — 較少監督下自我引導完成。
- **Innovation** — 對工作問題提出創意/替代方案。
- **Analytical Thinking** — 用邏輯分析處理工作議題。

→ 這 16 項即 O\*NET 官方「態度/軟性特質」原料,**每項有一句可觀察定義**,最適合當「態度欄」的權威清單。

### 8b. ESCO Transversal Knowledge, Skills and Competences(歐盟官方 transversal 清單)

**權威頁:** https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/escopedia/escopedia/transversal-knowledge-skills-and-competences
官方定義(原文):"Transversal knowledge, skills and competences are **relevant to a broad range of occupations and economic sectors**. They are often referred to as **core skills, basic skills or soft skills**…"

**六大類(Level 1,ESCO 修訂後模型,官方)——由「內在核心」到「外在社會情境」排列:**
1. **Core skills and competences**
2. **Thinking skills and competences**
3. **Self-management skills and competences**
4. **Social and communication skills and competences**
5. **Physical and manual skills and competences**
6. **Life skills and competences**
(三層結構:6 大類 → clusters(L2)→ 單一概念(L3)。另附 Green / DigComp / Research 標籤。來源:ESCO MSWG 專家組報告 *Towards a structured and consistent terminology on transversal skills*。)

### 8c. SFIA behavioural factors(見第 5a)

SFIA 的 12 項 business skills / behavioural factors(collaboration, communication, creativity, decision-making, digital mindset, leadership, learning & development, planning, problem-solving, adaptability, improvement mindset, security/privacy/ethics)也是官方軟技能清單,且**每項在每一級都有對應行為描述**(隨級升階),適合當「態度/軟技能 × 級別」二維教材。

## 對八個欄位各自可用的原料對照表

> 欄位對映到 Caliburn 的:任務 / 產出 / 行為指標 / 知識 / 技能 / 態度 / 級別(+ 職責duty 分組)

| Caliburn 欄位 | 最佳官方原料 | 可直接用的判準/句式 | 出處 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **任務(task)** | O\*NET *Task Writing Guidelines* | 三段式 Action>Object>Purpose;動詞開頭+複數現在式+具體;禁分號/e.g./i.e./and-or/in order to/時間相依;寧拆多條;「最小有意義產出單位」 | GreenTask_AppB.pdf |
| **職責/duty 分組** | UK NOS functional analysis | 一 NOS=一 function(可由一人執行);key purpose→main function→discrete function 三階;無數量規定 | NOS QC §3.2, §5.1 |
| **產出(deliverable)** | O\*NET Purpose/Result(內含) | **無獨立官方欄位**;最接近做法=任務的 "to + 可查核結果";產出寫成名詞化、可交付物件(Things/Data) | GreenTask_AppB;NOS §3.2 |
| **行為指標** | Bloom 修訂版 + NOS Behaviours | 用 19 個可觀察認知歷程動詞;**禁 understand/know/appreciate 等不可觀察動詞**(「more applicable to assessment than to learning」);NOS:可觀察的情境回應句 | Krathwohl 2002;Airasian & Miranda 2002;NOS §5.12 |
| **知識(knowledge)** | ESCO knowledge 定義 + Bloom 知識四型 | knowledge 寫**名詞化領域概念**(不加動詞、不加「知識/知道」);可分 factual/conceptual/procedural/metacognitive 細化 | ESCO escopedia;Krathwohl 2002 Table 2 |
| **技能(skill)** | ESCO skill 定義 + O\*NET Skills | skill=「apply knowledge & use know-how to complete tasks / solve problems」;用**動詞/動作短語**;分 cognitive/practical | ESCO escopedia;O\*NET Content Model |
| **態度(attitude)** | O\*NET Work Styles(16 項) + ESCO transversal(6 類) | 16 項各附一句可觀察定義;ESCO 六大 transversal 類;每項寫成可觀察傾向 | onetonline 1.C;ESCO transversal 頁 |
| **級別(level)** | SFIA 7 級 + EQF 8 級 | 沿四軸判級:autonomy / complexity(情境可預測性)/ influence(範圍)/ knowledge(深廣);逐級升階措辭 | SFIA how-it-works + L1–7;Europass EQF |
